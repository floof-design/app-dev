# The Amazing Spider-Man (2012)

> "We all have secrets: the ones we keep... and the ones that are kept from us."

## Overview
**The Amazing Spider-Man** is a superhero film directed by Marc Webb that serves as a reboot of the Spider-Man film franchise. It explores Peter Parker's teenage years in high school as he uncovers clues about his parents' mysterious disappearance while developing his superpowers.

### Key Details
* **Director:** Marc Webb
* **Release Year:** 2012
* **Genre:** Superhero / Action / Romance

### Main Characters
1. **Peter Parker / Spider-Man** (Andrew Garfield) - A witty, skateboard-riding outcast balancing high school science with vigilantism.
2. **Gwen Stacy** (Emma Stone) - Peter's brilliant classmate and love interest who works at Oscorp.
3. **Dr. Curt Connors / The Lizard** (Rhys Ifans) - A brilliant one-armed geneticist whose serum transforms him into a reptilian monster.
